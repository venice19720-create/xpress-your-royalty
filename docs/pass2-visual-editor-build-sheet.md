# Pass 2 — Visual Editor Conversion Build Sheet

Purpose: convert the current Wix Editor site into the strongest possible inquiry-driven experience without adding paid tools, migrating platforms, or increasing monthly cost.

## Global conversion rule

Primary CTA: **Start Your Event Inquiry** → HoneyBook.

Secondary CTA: **Explore Our Work** or page-specific service exploration.

Avoid competing booking routes, generic “Learn More” buttons, and duplicate CTAs that lead to different systems.

## Home

Target order:
1. Hero
2. Signature Services
3. Recent Work / visual proof
4. Why Xpress Your Royalty
5. Draping & Backdrops feature
6. Rentals feature
7. Your Event Journey
8. Verified client proof only
9. Final inquiry CTA

Hero:
- Keep H1: **Where Every Celebration Is Crowned With Elegance**
- Eyebrow: **EVENT DESIGN • DRAPING & BACKDROPS • DÉCOR • RENTALS**
- Supporting copy should immediately identify services, event types, and DE/PA/NJ/MD service region.
- Primary button: **Start Your Event Inquiry**
- Secondary button: **Explore Our Work**
- Use one strong authentic XYR image or installation view. Avoid a crowded image collage above the fold.

Visual hierarchy:
- Primary CTA must have the strongest visual weight.
- Do not use more than two competing button styles in the hero.
- Keep decorative animation minimal.

## Services

Purpose: help visitors self-select the service they need.

Primary service blocks:
- Event Design & Décor
- Draping & Backdrops
- Rentals & Event Support
- Social, Corporate & Community Events

Each block should answer:
- What it is
- What it helps accomplish
- Who it is best for
- What the next step is

Avoid full-service wedding-planner positioning.

## Draping & Backdrops

Treat as flagship service.

Preferred structure:
1. Strong installation image
2. Service promise
3. Uses: stages, sweetheart areas, ceremonies, photo moments, statement spaces, corporate/community focal areas
4. Real XYR draping examples
5. What determines scope: venue, dimensions, colors/materials, installation requirements
6. CTA: **Start Your Draping Inquiry**

Use authentic work wherever possible.

## Rentals

Preferred scan order:
- Tables & Chairs
- Draping & Backdrop Elements
- Linens & Table Styling
- Games & Interactive Rentals
- AV & Event Equipment
- Specialty Décor & Add-ons

Conversion notes:
- Clearly state availability varies by date, quantity, access, delivery logistics, and scope.
- Delivery only; no client pickup.
- First-floor delivery/access policy should be visible before commitment.
- CTA: **Check Rental Availability**

Do not leave “Coming Soon” visual blocks.

## About

Purpose: build trust without pretending XYR is a large agency.

Use:
- Founder-led, hands-on approach
- Intentional design and clear communication
- Insured business operations
- Service area
- Real work/process photography if available

CTA: **Start Your Event Inquiry**

## Contact / Inquiry

Keep the page focused on qualification rather than generic contact.

Ask visitors to prepare:
- Event type
- Date
- Venue/location
- Estimated guest count
- Services of interest
- Colors/style
- Inspiration
- Access or special requirements

Explain next step: review details, confirm fit/availability, then continue through HoneyBook.

## Navigation

Preferred public navigation:
- Home
- Services
- Draping & Backdrops
- Rentals
- Portfolio / Recent Work
- About
- Contact / Start Your Event Inquiry

Remove or hide legacy booking routes from public navigation when HoneyBook is the selected inquiry system.

Resolve duplicate Inventory routes intentionally; one public Rentals/Inventory destination only.

## Mobile QA for Pass 2

On each core page verify:
- Hero text does not wrap awkwardly
- Primary CTA appears before excessive scrolling
- Buttons meet comfortable touch size
- No overlapping text/images
- Gallery crops remain useful
- Service cards are readable without side-to-side scrolling
- Footer contact information is legible
- No legacy Wix booking CTA appears above HoneyBook CTA

## Pass 2 completion criteria

Pass 2 is complete when:
- One dominant inquiry path exists throughout the site
- Homepage tells visitors what XYR does within the first screen
- Draping is visibly elevated as a signature service
- Rentals are scannable and policy-aware
- About builds trust without inflated team claims
- Contact qualifies leads before inquiry
- Legacy booking routes are not promoted
- Mobile CTA hierarchy is clear
- No unverified testimonial is visible
