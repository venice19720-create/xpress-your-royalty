# Xpress Your Royalty — Homepage Build Specification

## Approved positioning
Lead with event design, draping/backdrops, rentals, and social/corporate celebrations. Keep selected planning/setup support and micro-weddings visible, but do not position XYR primarily as a large-wedding coordination company.

## Hero
### Headline
Where Every Celebration Is Crowned With Elegance

### Supporting line
Elevated event design, draping, backdrops, rentals, and celebration styling for social, corporate, community, and intimate wedding events across Delaware, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Maryland.

### Primary CTA
Start Your Event Inquiry

### Secondary CTA
Explore Our Work

### Visual direction
Use an authentic high-impact XYR setup image or short, subtle visual sequence sourced from approved public marketing content. Avoid stock imagery where strong owned work is available. Image must read clearly on mobile and allow sufficient contrast for headline/CTA text.

## Section 2 — Signature Services
Feature four primary cards:
- Event Design & Décor
- Draping & Backdrops
- Rentals
- Social & Corporate Celebrations

Each card should include one authentic XYR image, a concise value statement, and a service-detail CTA.

## Section 3 — Recent Work / Transformations
Curated visual proof using 6–10 authentic XYR images from approved Instagram and Facebook marketing content. Prioritize completed setups, draping details, before/after transformations, rental displays, community/corporate work, and polished social celebrations.

Do not rely on a live social feed as the only portfolio. Host selected optimized originals in Wix media. A smaller social module may link to Instagram for current updates.

## Section 4 — Why Choose XYR
Messaging pillars:
- Intentional, client-centered design
- Professional setup and breakdown support
- Flexible services for intimate and larger celebrations
- Regional service across DE/PA/NJ/MD
- In-house décor, draping, and selected rental inventory

Only include trust claims that can be verified.

## Section 5 — Client Journey
Recommended 4-step flow:
1. Share Your Vision
2. Consultation & Scope
3. Design / Planning & Preparation
4. Setup, Execution & Breakdown

Replace the generic “Post-Event Evaluation” emphasis on the homepage with a more client-relevant journey.

## Section 6 — Verified Social Proof
Use only real client reviews/testimonials. If the current Emily & Michael testimonial cannot be verified, remove it before the refreshed homepage is published.

## Section 7 — Social / Recent Updates
Optional compact section linking to Instagram @xpressyourroyaltyevents_. Use curated images rather than an oversized embedded feed if the feed adds performance or layout instability.

## Section 8 — Final CTA
Headline direction: Ready to Bring Your Vision to Life?

CTA: Start Your Event Inquiry

Route to the approved HoneyBook inquiry/consultation path. Avoid multiple competing forms or booking systems unless there is a clear business reason.

## Contact consistency
Public-facing email should use info@xpressyourroyalty.com where appropriate. Retain phone and service-area information only after verifying it is current.

## Visual system
- Preserve the established purple/gold royal identity, but use restraint so the site feels premium rather than decorative.
- Favor generous spacing, strong photography, clean typography, restrained gold accents, and consistent button styles.
- Avoid excessive animation. Motion should be subtle, accessible, and secondary to the content.
- Ensure sufficient color contrast and visible keyboard focus states.

## Mobile-first requirements
- Hero content readable without text covering important photo subjects.
- Primary CTA visible without excessive scrolling.
- Service cards stack cleanly.
- Images use responsive dimensions and compression.
- Tap targets remain comfortably usable.

## SEO/content requirements
- One clear H1 on the homepage.
- Use natural service/location language without keyword stuffing.
- Write descriptive alt text based on what is actually visible in each XYR image.
- Homepage title/meta should emphasize event design, décor, draping, rentals, and the primary regional market.
- Add relevant internal links to Services, Draping & Backdrops, Rentals/Inventory, Portfolio, About, and Contact.

## Conversion tracking requirements
Track at minimum:
- Primary hero inquiry clicks
- Secondary portfolio clicks
- Service-card clicks
- HoneyBook outbound/inquiry actions
- Phone/email clicks where applicable

## Benchmark rationale
Competitive review shows stronger Philadelphia-region event businesses make their specialties explicit, segment event types/services clearly, lead with authentic imagery, and repeat a simple contact path. Philadelphia Event Decor separates Weddings, Mitzvahs, Social, and Corporate and prominently frames its full-service capabilities; Bobby Morganstein Events leads with event design, rentals, and production; Lens Society Events explicitly groups Weddings, Corporate, Social, Private Events, and photo booth services. The XYR homepage should adopt the clarity and proof structure without copying competitor wording or overstating XYR's scale.

## Publication gate
Do not publish the refreshed homepage until:
- Hero copy and imagery are approved.
- Contact information is correct.
- CTAs resolve to the intended HoneyBook path.
- Every testimonial is verified.
- Mobile layout is reviewed.
- Accessibility basics are checked.
- Images are optimized.
- No placeholder / “coming soon” content undermines credibility.
