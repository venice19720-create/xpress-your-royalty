# Xpress Your Royalty — Service Architecture & Website Copy

## Positioning
Xpress Your Royalty should present first as an event design, draping, décor, and rental company with selected planning/setup support—not primarily as a large-wedding coordination company.

## Core brand promise
**Your Vision. Your Vibe. Your Royalty.**

Supporting positioning:
Xpress Your Royalty transforms celebrations through thoughtful event design, custom draping and backdrops, décor, rentals, and polished setup support for social, corporate, community, and intimate wedding events throughout Delaware, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Maryland.

## Additional Service Pathways

### Micro-Weddings & Intimate Celebrations
For couples planning an intimate wedding or smaller celebration, XYR can provide décor, draping, backdrops, rentals, styling, and selected setup/planning support based on scope. Avoid promising full-scale wedding coordination unless specifically contracted.

### Planning & Setup Support
For qualifying events, XYR can help organize décor-related details, layout considerations, setup sequencing, and event-day installation/breakdown responsibilities. Scope must be clearly defined in the proposal and contract.

### Mobile Photo Experience
Present as a supporting experiential service rather than a replacement for professional wedding/event photography. Use the approved `preview gallery` language where relevant.

## Why Choose XYR
Use only claims that can be supported.

Recommended proof themes:
- Personalized event vision and consultation
- Multi-service capability: design, draping, décor, rentals, setup support
- Delivery-focused logistics
- Service across DE / PA / NJ / MD subject to project scope
- Insured business / appropriate verified credentials where publicly suitable
- Community-event experience
- Clear proposal, contract, and payment process

Avoid invented metrics, awards, client counts, or unverifiable `premier`/`best` claims.

## Client Journey
### 1. Share Your Vision
Submit event details through the approved inquiry form.

### 2. Consultation & Scope
Discuss priorities, venue, guest experience, design needs, logistics, and budget expectations.

### 3. Proposal & Booking
Receive the defined scope, pricing, agreement, and payment schedule.

### 4. Design & Preparation
Finalize approved details, sourcing, rentals, measurements, and production logistics.

### 5. Installation & Event Support
XYR completes the contracted delivery, setup, styling, installation, and/or event support.

### 6. Breakdown / Return
Where contracted, the team completes strike, breakdown, and rental return logistics.

## CTA System
Use a limited consistent CTA vocabulary:
- Start Your Event Inquiry
- Book Your Consultation
- Explore Our Work
- Explore Draping & Backdrops
- Browse Rental Categories

Avoid mixing many generic labels such as Learn More / Click Here / Submit / Get Started when a more descriptive action is available.

## Copy quality rules
- Specific over generic.
- Warm and elevated, not exaggerated.
- No unsupported superlatives.
- No promises outside actual contract scope.
- Authentic XYR photography accompanies major claims.
- Keep paragraphs short and scannable on mobile.
- Use location terms naturally, never keyword-stuff.
- Every service page should lead to a clear inquiry action.
